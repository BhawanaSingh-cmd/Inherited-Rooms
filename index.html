Welcome

The objects in this room were collected between 1987 and 2004 and are preserved here for Heritage Fest 2071. Look around. Open whatever catches your eye. Take your time.

  • LookDrag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Arrow keys work too.
  • OpenTap a glowing ring to open an artefact and read its label.
  • ReturnClose an artefact with × or Esc, then keep exploring.

There is no set order, and no right answer.
