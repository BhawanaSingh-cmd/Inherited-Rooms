Artefact K7

Sil-batta, grinding stone

Oral testimonyTranscribed
“शादी में इसी सिल पे पैर रखवाया था। पत्थर जैसी टिकना।”

“Shaadi mein isi sil pe pair rakhwaya tha. Patthar jaisi tikna.”

At the wedding, she was made to step on this very stone. Stay put, like stone.

Ashmarohan: in Hindu weddings the bride places her foot on a grinding stone, asked to be as steady as stone in her new home. Said to her on her first morning in the kitchen, 1987.

Collected 1987 · Carved stone; masala; alta
