Storage jars

Jaapa (जापा): the confinement after childbirth

The forty days after a birth, when the new mother rests at home and is fed special “warming” foods. Gond (edible gum) and ajwain are made into laddoos and kaadha for the new mother.

Collected 1991 · Glass jars, edible gum and ajwain; ink on tape
